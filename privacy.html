Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

Hewn does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you log stays on your device.

Data storage

All data — including protein and movement logs, goals, and daily history — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

No accounts

Hewn requires no account, email address, or personal information to use. There is no registration or login.

No analytics or tracking

We do not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or any other third-party tracking. We have no way of knowing how many people use the app or what they log.

No third parties

We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information in the first place.

Fonts

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your device requests fonts, Google's servers receive your IP address as part of a standard web request. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle this.

Data deletion

To delete all your data, clear your browser's site data for this app, or uninstall the app from your device. This permanently removes everything that was stored locally.

Children

Hewn does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing specific to children to address.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above. Since we collect no data, significant changes are unlikely.
