## Description (max 4000 chars)

Hewn is a minimal daily tracker for protein and movement — the two habits that quietly compound into feeling better.

**How it works**

Log protein by piece, scoop, or glass — not by gram. An egg is an egg. A scoop of Greek yogurt is a scoop. The math happens invisibly so you can focus on eating, not calculating.

Log movement in minutes. A walk counts. A swim counts. A class counts. Go over your goal and it just means you did great — there's no cap.

**Your character**

As you log through the day, a small character on screen gets progressively happier — from flat and grumpy in the morning to glowing and rosy-cheeked once you've hit your targets.

**Your shelf**

Recent days are kept in a quiet archive called the Shelf. Tap any day to see what you logged. No charts, no streaks pressure — just a calm record of how the week went.

**Everything stays on your device**

No account. No cloud. No subscription. Your data lives locally and goes nowhere. Clear your data anytime by clearing the app's storage.

**For people who want just enough**

Hewn is not a calorie counter. It doesn't track macros, weight, or water. It does two things well and gets out of the way.
